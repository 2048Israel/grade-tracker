**What's new in 0.1.2**

- After an in-app update, Grade Tracker opens in front of your other windows and confirms it: *"Updated to Grade Tracker v0.1.2. Your courses and marks are all here."* The version number at the top right shows a ✓ for a few seconds.

Already on 0.1.1? Click the green **⬆ Update** button at the top right (or click the version number to check now). On 0.1.0, download this one by hand once; after that, updates are one click.

**New here?** Download `GradeTracker.exe` below and run it. There's nothing to install. Windows only. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**: the app isn't code-signed. It's built from this repository's source by GitHub Actions.

1. Click **Add course → Copy AI prompt**.
2. Paste the prompt into your AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot…) and attach your syllabus: the PDF, screenshots, or photos.
3. Paste its reply back into Grade Tracker, check what it read, and click **Add**.

Your data stays on your computer, in `%APPDATA%\Grade Tracker`.
