**Download `GradeTracker.exe` below and run it.** There's nothing to install. Windows only.

If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**: the app isn't code-signed. It's built from this repository's source by GitHub Actions.

Getting started:

1. Click **Add course → Copy AI prompt**.
2. Paste the prompt into your AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot…) and attach your syllabus: the PDF, screenshots, or photos.
3. Paste its reply back into Grade Tracker, check what it read, and click **Add**.

Your data stays on your computer, in `%APPDATA%\Grade Tracker`.
